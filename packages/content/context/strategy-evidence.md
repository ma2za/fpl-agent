# Strategy Evidence

Use this note as evidence for the season plan and weekly strategy. It is not a player-selection script.

## Evidence-Backed Principles

- Keep the initial squad balanced across positions. Official FPL guidance highlights the risk of overloading attack and leaving the defence underfunded.
- Use early-season ownership carefully. Official guidance says popular players are often proven picks and avoiding too many of them can create early catch-up risk.
- Plan around fixture runs. FPL Fixture Difficulty Rating is explicitly intended to support transfer and team-selection planning.
- Leave some money in the bank. Official guidance suggests a small bank can help react to early price movement and emerging players.
- Be patient with transfers. The 2025/26 overall winner took no points hits, rolled frequently, and used saved transfers for larger planned moves.
- Keep captaincy relatively safe. The 2025/26 overall winner concentrated captaincy on reliable premiums and treated poor captaincy as a major gameweek-risk source.
- Save chips for clear structural edges. Official FPL chip guidance points to double and blank gameweeks for Free Hit, Bench Boost, and Triple Captain value.
- Use Free Hit defensively when the squad is weak for a blank or awkward week, not just as an aggressive one-week attack.
- Account for 2026/27 defensive contributions. Centre-backs and defensive midfielders with repeatable defensive actions can now carry more value than older FPL heuristics imply.
- Account for 2026/27 BPS changes. Goalkeepers, full-backs, and attacking players may gain bonus potential, while centre-back CBI overlap is reduced.

## Source Notes

- Official FPL basics confirm £100.0m budget, 15-player squad, 2/5/5/3 structure, max 3 per club, fixture planning, ownership caution, and leaving £0.5m-£1.0m bank.
- Official FPL 2026/27 launch confirms FPL is live, prices are available, unlimited transfers before GW1, two chip sets, roll up to five free transfers, no extra AFCON transfers this season, BPS tweaks, and later lockdown.
- Official FPL chip guidance confirms two sets of Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, and Bench Boost; first set expires before the GW19 deadline; only one chip can be active in a gameweek; Free Hit cannot be played in GW1.
- Official FPL defensive-contribution guidance confirms defenders earn 2 points at 10 CBIT, midfielders/forwards earn 2 points at 12 CBIRT, and the award is capped at 2 points per match.
- Official FPL BPS guidance confirms 2026/27 tweaks: tackled penalty removed, CBI BPS reduced, and goalkeeper save scoring changed.
- Premier League interview with 2025/26 champion Erik Ibsen supports patient transfers, no hits, planning in blocks, safe captaincy, and coordinated chip usage around doubles/blanks.
