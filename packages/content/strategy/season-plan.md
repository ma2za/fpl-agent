# Season Strategy

Status: agent_authored

## Defaults

- Risk profile: balanced
- Squad source: from-scratch GW1 squad until a real squad is configured
- Agent role: Codex authors decisions; scripts prepare evidence and checks

## Season Windows

- GW1-6: opening structure and information gathering.
- GW7-12: first fixture-swing adjustment window.
- GW13-20: winter congestion flexibility.
- GW21-30: chip and doubles/blanks preparation.
- GW31-38: run-in aggression and chip execution.

## Chip Posture

Conservative early. Save chips until fixture doubles, blanks, or exceptional captaincy edges are visible.

## Transfer Discipline

Balanced. Prefer rolling when structure is sound; take hits only for clear multi-week gains or unavailable players.

## Captaincy Policy

Prioritize reliable premium attackers with strong fixture, minutes, and role security.

## Squad-Building Principles

- Start with a legal, flexible squad structure.
- Keep captaincy routes open across fixture swings.
- Avoid weak benches that force early transfers.
- Treat current team news as mandatory before deadline.
